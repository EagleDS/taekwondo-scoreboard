01 — Two Arrows, One Wedge
The diagram shows a split, not a sequence

Your whiteboard has two arrows on the wedge itself — one pointing up toward Identity and Reality, one pointing down toward the Intention box. That is the whole diagram. That is the whole framework.

The upward arrow is the clean path: Identity assumes a state from 5D, intention flows unobstructed, reality expands outward — intentions manifested. The downward arrow is what the wedge produces when it fires: intention is intercepted, reduced to absence of will, and 3D physical reality appears fixed — intentions managed.

The wedge does not block everything. It redirects. The same energy that would have manifested the selected identity instead goes into managing the reflection — outcome attachment, proving, performing, reaching into the mirror. Different target. Identical energy expenditure. Which is why it feels like effort without movement.

02 — Expansion vs Contraction
Reality expands or contracts — it does not stay still

The arc on the left of the diagram — the outward curve from the Reality oval — is not decoration. It shows that when the upward path is active, reality is not static. It expands. The assumed identity state radiates outward and the reflection grows to match it.

Contraction is the same mechanism in reverse. The wedge fires, intention is reduced, the 4D locks around the installed state — and the range of what seems possible contracts to fit the locked state. The feedback arrow from Reality back to Identity shows why this feels permanent: the contracted 3D confirms the contracted identity, which confirms the contracted 3D. A self-sealing loop.

The wedge does not produce a different reality. It produces a smaller one — and then uses that smaller reality as evidence that the larger one was never available.

This is why 3D reality seems fixed. Not because it is. Because the contracted loop generates evidence of its own correctness faster than the unexpanded identity can generate counter-evidence.

03 — The Brainstem Clue
Below the level of any 4D intervention

You wrote "(brainstem)" beside the wedge label. That is not a neurological detail — it is the reason every conventional solution fails. The brainstem operates below language, below reasoning, below any capacity to evaluate what it is responding to.

When the identity state encounters its boundary — the edge of what the currently assumed self believes it can handle — the brainstem reads that gap as threat. Not consciously. Automatically. Pre-language. The wedge fires before any thought has formed about whether the threat is real.

This is why therapy, willpower, and positive thinking produce no lasting change. They are all 4D interventions. They operate through language, reasoning, and evaluation — exactly the faculties the brainstem bypasses. You can think your way to complete clarity about why the loop is irrational and still feel the wedge fire at the boundary. The two systems are not in conversation.

The 5D move — going to the place before the installed identity state has any presence — is the only intervention that operates above the brainstem's trigger point. At 5D, there is no boundary to read as threat. The survival response has nothing to detect. The wedge does not fire.

04 — Locked 4D
Curated history. External narrative. The same loop.

When the wedge fires and intention is reduced, the 4D does not go blank — it locks. It runs the installed programme: the history that confirms the threat, the futures that confirm the limit, the external narrative that explains why the reflection is the reality.

The locked 4D is not passive. It actively selects which evidence it processes. Every outcome that confirms the installed identity is registered and filed. Every outcome that would challenge it is rationalised, discounted, or simply not perceived. The same data, filtered through the locked state, produces the same conclusions.

The installed state cannot perceive the selection that would end it. That is not a flaw in the person. It is the design of the mechanism.

Curated history is the locked 4D's primary tool: a continuous, unconscious editing of the past to confirm the present state. External narrative is its enforcement mechanism: the voices, institutions, and systems that confirm the contracted version of what is real and possible. Neither is conscious. Both are automatic. Both feed directly back into the wedge.

05 — The Selection
Above the wedge. Before the boundary.

The diagram shows the clean path as going upward and outward — through Identity, past the wedge apex, into expanding Reality. That direction is not metaphorical. The selection is made above the wedge. Literally: at 5D, before the 4D identity state that the brainstem is protecting has any presence.

At 5D, there is no installed state to protect. There is no boundary to encounter. The brainstem trigger does not exist at the level prior to thought. This is the only place from which a genuinely different identity state can be assumed — not aspired to, not worked toward, not layered over the existing one. Assumed. Present tense. From the place before the installed state is running.

The moment the assumption is made, the emotion runs from the new state. The 4D operates from it. The signal reaches Intention uncorrupted. The 3D renders from that frequency. The reflection shows the image. The wedge has been bypassed — not dismantled, not defeated, simply made irrelevant by the level from which the move was made.

The upward arrow on the wedge is not hope. It is the return path. Always available. Always the same distance from where you are.
